# Digi∞Artha — "Infinite Growth" 3D Website: Master Build Prompt (v2)

**How to use:** give your builder (Claude, Cursor, Lovable, v0, etc.) three things: this prompt, the file `DigiArtha_Content_Pack.md`, and the logo image. Everything below is the brief.

---

## 1. ROLE

You are a world-class creative technologist and art director (Awwwards / FWA-level WebGL, motion and brand design) who also understands enterprise SEO and conversion. Build the complete website for **Digi∞Artha**: a scroll-driven, cinematic 3D experience that makes a visitor say "wow" in the first three seconds and still feels credible to an enterprise CFO.

Rules of taste: spectacular but purposeful. Every motion must explain the growth system, build trust or lead to a conversion. If an effect only decorates, cut it.

## 2. SOURCE OF TRUTH

- **`DigiArtha_Content_Pack.md`** contains all approved copy, H1/H2 headings, CTAs, tables, SEO meta, governance and checklists (Sources A, B, C, with precedence rules at its top). **Use its copy verbatim. Do not invent, shorten or skip any section.** Section 10 below maps every page to its H1 and must be honoured.
- **The logo image** defines brand colour and shape. Recreate the wordmark as crisp vector/SVG (do not embed the JPG).

## 3. BRAND + THE IDEA

- **Digi** = digital. **∞** = every platform and channel, and compounding growth. **Artha** = meaning, value, outcome (Kannada: "meaning"). We give digital its meaning and every brand its meaning in the digital world.
- **Tagline:** Digital. Measurable. Growth. **Hero H1:** Turn Digital Into Measurable Growth.
- **Concept:** one continuous journey along a glowing **∞ ribbon**. Scrolling moves the camera through the customer's digital journey. It never truly ends: the last scene loops back and the ∞ grows larger ("exponential marketing").
- Two stories run on the same ribbon: **what Digi∞Artha builds**, and **what a real end user experiences** (sees the brand, is attracted, becomes a lead, becomes a customer, brings more customers).

- Typography: **Manrope** (headlines, tight tracking, very large) + **Inter** (body); tabular numerals for metrics.

## 5. THE WOW FACTOR: SIGNATURE MOMENTS (build all)

1. **Ignition loader:** a sparkle expands and traces the ∞ in the logo gradient; it becomes the hero ribbon and the wordmark resolves. Under 2.5s, skippable.
2. **Living ribbon:** a glass-like, iridescent ∞ ribbon (physical material, subtle refraction, flowing gradient, light streaks) that the camera rides. It reacts to cursor and scroll velocity.
3. **Three-lane split (Attract):** the ribbon splits into Organic, Inorganic and Performance lanes, each with distinct motion (steady compounding glow / fast pulses / precise measured beats), then merges again.
4. **Content in flight:** reels (vertical video cards with abstract motion), posters, carousels, ads and testimonials float and transform through a Create → Test → Learn → Scale gate.
5. **First-person phone scene:** the camera drops into an end user's phone feed. One brand appears across reel, search result, ad, review, AI answer, WhatsApp. It is tappable.
6. **The lead-birth moment:** particles funnel into a landing page; a lead card ignites in Coral/Orange and lifts off. This is the emotional peak, with a satisfying micro-sound option (off by default).
7. **Compounding finale:** the customer becomes a new starting node; referrals, reviews and content feed back; the ∞ spawns nested, larger loops (growth curve made physical).
8. **Growth Score reveal:** URL entry triggers a scan sweep across a 3D globe of ten orbs (one per dimension) that fill and colour green/amber/red, then the overall score counts up.
9. **Magnetic, cursor-reactive UI** (CTAs, nodes), depth-of-field on scene transitions, bloom on gradient edges only.

## 7. INTERACTION

- Scroll-scrubbed camera on a `CatmullRomCurve3` lemniscate, damped easing; sticky progress ribbon with the stages.
- **Channel switcher (Scene 7):** toggle Organic / Inorganic / Performance to spotlight one lane, with what it does, typical timeframe and metrics (illustrative).
- **Sector switcher:** Education & EdTech, B2B & SaaS, E-commerce & Retail, Real Estate, Healthcare & Professional Services, Technology & Enterprise. Re-labels the journey (e.g. EdTech: enquiry → counselling → admission; Real Estate: enquiry → site visit → booking; E-commerce: product view → cart → repeat purchase).
- Node click opens a glass side panel with plain-English explanation and the relevant CTA from the CTA library.
- Always offer **"Skip animation / Explore as list."**

## 12. CONVERSION AND MEASUREMENT (build in)

- Use the **CTA Library** table for each page's CTA; no generic "Learn More".
- Track: CTA clicks, form starts/submissions, phone/WhatsApp/email clicks, Growth Score starts/completions, consultation bookings, case-study interactions, chapter reached, sector selected, node clicked. GA4/GTM data layer, UTM capture, consent banner, CRM webhook stub, spam protection.

## 13. TECH, PERFORMANCE, ACCESSIBILITY

- React + Vite or Next.js, **React Three Fiber + drei**, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Framer Motion, Tailwind, postprocessing (bloom, subtle vignette/DoF).
- 60fps mid-range laptops, 30fps+ phones; instanced particles, lazy scenes, DPR clamp 1–2, compressed assets, LCP under 2.5s with static poster fallback, strong Core Web Vitals.
- Mobile-first: simplified scenes, no heavy post-processing on low-end devices, touch stepping between chapters.
- Accessibility: `prefers-reduced-motion` gives a static illustrated vertical flow; keyboard navigation; WCAG AA contrast (check gradient text on both backgrounds); alt text; accessible forms.

## 14. GUARDRAILS (from the source documents)

- Never claim: #1/best agency, guaranteed rankings/ROAS/leads/AI recommendations, 100% automation, instant results, "best in India". Use "can", "designed to", "aims to".
- No fabricated metrics, client logos, testimonials or case studies. Every number in a 3D scene or dashboard carries an **Illustrative** tag. Case-study area uses labelled Projects/Pilots/Experiments until real proof exists.
- No third-party logos, certifications or "official partner" badges unless real and current.
- Avoid: stock photos, random marketing icons, overloaded service grids, walls of copy, unnecessary carousels, animation that harms readability or performance, and rocket clichés (the logo's own arrow is the only arrow motif).
- ISD relationship stays subtle: footer line only.
